# **DAY 1 教育目的**

### **1. 雅思写作题目**

Some students take one year off between finishing school and going to university, in order to travel or to work. Do you think advantages outweigh disadvantages?

**中文翻译：**

一些学生在完成中学学业后、进入大学之前，会休学一年，用来旅行或工作。你认为这样做的利大于弊还是弊大于利？

### **2. 思维导图：休学一年利弊**

**利（Advantages）**

1. 独立处理问题 → 提升独立性
2. 接触多元文化人群 → 拓宽视野
3. 积累工作经验 → 奠基职业

**弊（Disadvantages）**

1. 学术进度延迟 → 返校适应难
2. 经济压力（自食其力或依赖家庭）

利（Advantages）
在这期间，学生需要独立处理各种问题，从而提高个人的自我成长和独立性
1 
During this time , students need to deal with various issues independently, thereby improving their personal growth and independence
2 
Through traveling or working , students can be exposed to different cultures and people, thus broadening their horizons and 
life perspectives.
3.
in this year, students can gain valuable practical experience through actual work, laying the foundation for their future career.

弊（Disadvantages）
1. 
Students may experience a delay in their academic progress due to taking a year off, making it difficult for them to readjust to academic 
evnironment upon their return.
2.
during this year, students may need to support themselves through work or rely on their parents for financial assistance, whitch can be a 
addition burden for them.
